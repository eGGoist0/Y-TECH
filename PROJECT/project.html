AQUAPURE

Our project is called "AQUAPURE". Our project will purify and extract minerals
from ocean water, neutralize high levels of PH and not only from ocean water.
We will use several filters, such as: a mechanical filter for rough cleaning,
a special powder for neutralizing high PH and a reverse osmosis system.

MODEL :

Filters

Motors

PH Sensor

At this stage, we perform rough filtration of collecting water. We use a mechanical filter (1) at this stage.

At this stage, we add special compounds to separate minerals from the water. PH sensors and motors are used to monitor and mix the solution effectively.

At this stage, we filter water from least minerals and remove all excess elements with filter, which we have created by ourselfs (2,3). Then filter (3) separate water and minerals from each other.

At this stage, we filter water from different bacterias, virus and salt by system of reverse osmosis (4). Then this water we collect in the special container.
