# Y-TECH Website

## Description
This website introduces the Y-TECH team and its work for the Central Asia Competition. It gives visitors and judges a quick overview of our team, goals, and project.

## Website Structure
- **Main Page** — A brief introduction to our research goals.
- **About Us** — Information about our team members.
- **Project** — An overview of our project, its components, and their functions.
- **Quality** — A tool for calculating how much of a specific compound is needed for different conditions.
